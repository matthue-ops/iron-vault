Supported formats and limitations

Use the app’s unmodified .xlsx export, an Overview-only workbook, or a CSV with the same label/value rows. XLS, encrypted workbooks and formula-based metrics are unsupported. All dates use Philippine time. No daily membership history or tier-grace verification is inferred.
